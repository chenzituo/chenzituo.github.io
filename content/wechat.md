**We Happy Stones** is my Chinese fiction account on WeChat. The Chinese name, **与子同石**, brings together “与子同袍” from the *Book of Songs*, the 子 in my name, and the stone of *The Story of the Stone* (红楼梦): you and I, both stones.

The English name echoes Shakespeare’s “we few, we happy few, we band of brothers” in [*Henry V*](https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/henry-v/read/4/3/)—a sense of fellowship, of being fortunate to find ourselves together.
